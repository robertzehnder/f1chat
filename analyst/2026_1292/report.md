# Norris passes Antonelli and Hamilton to win at Zandvoort

Lando Norris won the Dutch Grand Prix by 11.536 seconds from Kimi Antonelli, with George Russell completing the podium. A longer middle stint put Norris on fresher hard tyres for his decisive attack on lap 54; the subsequent virtual safety car widened his advantage when Antonelli stopped and he stayed out.

It was Norris’s second successive victory after Hungary, secured despite losing the lead at the restart. Mercedes took both remaining podium places after ordering its drivers to swap, with Russell then holding off the Ferraris.

Before the start, [Luke Smith noted the contrast between the earlier rain and the field’s choice of slicks](https://x.com/i/web/status/2091510131922014275). Norris held his lead from pole, but Max Verstappen’s final home race ended on lap 1 when he [lost control at the still-damp final corner and struck the barriers](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-reacts-to-dramatic-dutch-gp-crash-as-he-sums-up-disappointing-home-weekend.6nrEOE03YhjdJy6shRzAf1). Verstappen later said he was physically okay. The red flag began on lap 2.

The restart preparations brought confusion at pit exit. [Chris Medland described cars near the back being held by a red light while Norris was already approaching the grid](https://x.com/i/web/status/2091519800912605247). Once racing resumed, Antonelli took the lead from Norris, while Piastri [passed Russell](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/you-want-to-fight-for-every-single-position-russell-offers-verdict-on-mercedes-team-orders-in-dutch-gp.3qBtPSF2Jgfyu63IKV8our). [Antonelli credited his starts with salvaging his race](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-details-what-saved-mercedes-as-he-admits-being-disappointed-by-p2-in-dutch-grand-prix.7Cebf84txGCxavWTdiKBs4), having also overtaken Russell at the initial getaway.

The suspension had split the tyre choices: Norris switched from mediums to softs, Piastri from mediums to hards, while both Mercedes drivers retained mediums. Hamilton and Charles Leclerc stayed on softs. McLaren’s drivers were now pursuing the Mercedes pair on different compounds.

Russell stopped for hards on lap 17, with Piastri following on lap 18. Piastri spent 20.2 seconds in the pit lane and [lost the position to Russell through a slow stop](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-race-day-in-the-netherlands-2026.1GTy1x6hObFlkGkaXKqhNS). Grip difficulties followed, and both Ferraris passed him.

Antonelli and Norris took hards on lap 21, leaving Hamilton out front before his own stop on lap 25 returned Antonelli to the lead. [Norris said the softs had left him struggling for pace and balance, whereas the hards allowed him to push](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-race-day-in-the-netherlands-2026.1GTy1x6hObFlkGkaXKqhNS). He credited that improvement with forcing Mercedes to stop early.

Both Mercedes drivers fitted fresh hards on lap 40, but Norris stayed out. Hamilton also extended his stint, after [voicing frustration over the radio about time spent behind Leclerc](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/i-want-to-win-hamilton-explains-frustrated-radio-messages-in-dutch-grand-prix.4mMvpLPKV7T2kfnkITCYuO). Hamilton said he believed he had more pace and explained that his impatience came from wanting to win.

Norris finally stopped on lap 47, taking another hard set in a pit-lane time of 17.5 seconds. Hamilton led at the end of lap 48, with Antonelli between the Ferrari and the McLaren. Norris had surrendered track position, but his tyres were fresher than Antonelli’s.

Norris rapidly closed on the Mercedes, reducing its advantage to a fraction of a second. Hamilton was still ahead of both, so catching Antonelli alone wouldn’t secure the lead. [Norris later credited McLaren’s strategy with giving him fresh tyres to attack while Antonelli was occupied with Hamilton](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/what-the-teams-said-race-day-in-the-netherlands-2026.1GTy1x6hObFlkGkaXKqhNS).

On lap 54, Norris [went around Antonelli’s outside at the opening corner, then caught and passed Hamilton on the same lap](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/we-needed-to-put-on-a-show-norris-dissects-the-move-that-won-me-the-final-dutch-gp.6fS1X9q1vngxLevmcyQan4). By the line, Antonelli was 1.2 seconds behind him. Norris’s longer stint had delivered the tyre advantage; the overtakes converted it into the lead.

The virtual safety car began on lap 55 after [Ocon’s Haas stopped on track](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-wins-dramatic-dutch-grand-prix-from-antonelli-and-russell-as-verstappen-crashes-out.Zn7iYevVGp5eHzFkTEAz7). Hamilton, Leclerc and Piastri took softs, followed by Antonelli on lap 56, while Norris and Russell stayed out. Antonelli rejoined behind Russell, and by the end of lap 57 his deficit to Norris was 12.6 seconds. [Antonelli said his final hard-tyre stint had brought little confidence and insufficient pace](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/antonelli-details-what-saved-mercedes-as-he-admits-being-disappointed-by-p2-in-dutch-grand-prix.7Cebf84txGCxavWTdiKBs4), but the softs couldn’t restore his challenge for victory.

Mercedes then instructed Russell to let Antonelli through. [Toto Wolff said the team wanted to avoid its drivers costing each other time with Ferrari closing in](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-logical-consequence-wolff-explains-mercedes-decision-to-invert-cars-in-dutch-grand-prix.26VGrypPUwwLaL0mBocjmF), and pointed out that Antonelli had been ahead before stopping. Russell initially questioned the order and warned that it threatened the double podium, but [accepted afterwards that Antonelli deserved to finish ahead and had the tyres to pass anyway](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/you-want-to-fight-for-every-single-position-russell-offers-verdict-on-mercedes-team-orders-in-dutch-gp.3qBtPSF2Jgfyu63IKV8our).

A further virtual safety car began and ended on lap 70. [Chris Medland linked it to debris from the Williams collision](https://x.com/i/web/status/2091542375856287768), and [judged that the interruption might have preserved Russell’s podium with Hamilton pressing him](https://x.com/i/web/status/2091542550498722225). Russell finished ahead of Hamilton and Leclerc, with Piastri behind the Ferraris.

Antonelli leaves Zandvoort leading the championship on 242 points, 59 clear of Russell. Norris is fourth on 159, still 83 behind Antonelli despite consecutive wins. The Italian Grand Prix at Monza is next.

## About the data

Timing and race-control data come from OpenF1, compiled into the packet linked at the foot of the page. Gaps are line-crossing differences at each lap’s end, not broadcast intervals. The gap chart’s final point is reconstructed from lap start plus duration, and positions are carried forward between logged changes.

The red flag’s end and the lap-3 safety car’s end are inferred. Causes, corners and all remarks come from the linked reporting. Pit-lane times shown are lane times, not net loss.
